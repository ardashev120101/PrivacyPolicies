# Peek A Duo

A peek-a-boo picture book for little ones, made for iPhone Duo and every iPhone.

## Support

Questions, problems, or ideas? Email **ruslan@alumni.duke.edu** and I'll get back to you.

**How to read:** pick a book, then close the phone (or hide it from your little one) and
open it again to reveal the next page. On iPhones without a fold, turn the screen away or
cover it with your hand.

## Privacy

Peek A Duo collects nothing. Read the full [privacy policy](privacy).
