# Peek A Duo Privacy Policy

*Effective November 3, 2026*

Peek A Duo is a peek-a-boo picture book for young children. It was made to collect nothing
about the people who use it.

## The short version

- Peek A Duo does not collect, store, or share any personal information.
- It has no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking, or in-app purchases.
- It never connects to the internet.
- The camera is used only on the last page, only while that page is on screen, and
  nothing it sees is recorded, saved, or sent anywhere.

## The camera

The last page of every book, "Peek a YOU!", shows the reader's own face in a rainbow
mirror using the front camera. To do this, the app asks for permission to use the camera
the first time that page appears.

- Camera images are shown live on screen and then discarded. They are never recorded,
  photographed, saved, analyzed, or transmitted.
- The camera turns off as soon as the reader leaves the last page. iOS shows its camera
  indicator whenever the camera is on.
- If permission is not given, the page shows an illustrated baby in the mirror instead,
  and the rest of the book works the same.
- You can change camera permission at any time in **Settings > Peek A Duo > Camera**.

## Information we collect

None. Peek A Duo does not collect names, contact details, photos, location, device
identifiers, usage data, or diagnostics. It contains no third-party code that does.

The app keeps no record of what has been read.

## Children's privacy

Peek A Duo is designed for children. Because it collects no personal information from
anyone, it collects none from children, consistent with the Children's Online Privacy
Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws. It contains no links out of the app, no ads, and
no purchases.

## Information shared with Apple

Apple may provide developers with aggregate, anonymous App Store and crash information
when you have chosen to share it with app developers in your device's settings. This is
controlled by you and by Apple's privacy policy, not by Peek A Duo.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new
effective date. If a future version of Peek A Duo ever needs to collect information, the
policy will be updated before that version is released.
