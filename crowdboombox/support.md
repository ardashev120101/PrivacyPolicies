# CrowdBoombox

Turn everyone's phone into one big speaker: one phone plays the music, and every phone
that joins plays it at the same moment.

was doing.

## Common questions

**A friend's party doesn't show up.** Keep Wi-Fi switched on for every phone (you don't need
to join a network) and stay close together. If you tapped Don't Allow when asked about
devices on your local network, turn it back on in Settings → Privacy & Security → Local
Network → CrowdBoombox.

**Some of my songs can't be played.** CrowdBoombox plays songs saved on your device, bought
from the iTunes Store or synced from a computer. Songs from a streaming subscription, such
as Apple Music, and songs still in iCloud can't be shared.

**Playing from a cable.** Connect an iPod, an MP3 player or a DJ mixer to the host phone with
a USB-C audio adapter, then tap the Aux-In tile.

**Restoring a court.** Open Settings → Look → Court and tap Restore Purchases.
