# CrowdBoombox Privacy Policy

*Effective November 7, 2026*

CrowdBoombox plays the same music on nearby phones at the same moment. It was made to
collect nothing about the people who use it.

## The short version

- CrowdBoombox does not collect, store, or share any personal information with us or
  anyone else.
- It has no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking.
- Music and party information travel only between the phones in a party, nearby. They
  are never sent to us or to any server.
- Optional tips and court purchases are handled entirely by Apple.

## Nearby phones and the local network

To find and join parties, CrowdBoombox uses the local network and direct connections
between nearby phones. iOS asks for permission the first time.

- A phone hosting or relaying a party shows its display name to nearby phones running
  CrowdBoombox, so they can find the party. You choose the name; it doesn't have to be
  your real one.
- Phones in a party exchange what's needed to play in sync: the music, the queue, display
  names, and technical details such as timing, connection quality, and whether a phone is
  charging and how full its battery is. This stays between the phones in the party.
- You can change local network permission at any time in
  **Settings > CrowdBoombox > Local Network**.

## Your music

When you host, CrowdBoombox reads only the songs you choose from your Music library and
streams them to the phones in your party. iOS asks for permission first.

- Music is only ever streamed. No phone keeps a lasting copy of your songs.
- While a party is playing, each phone holds a temporary cache of what it's playing, so
  everyone stays in sync and a new host can take over smoothly. It isn't a song file you
  can open, save, or share.
- That cache is deleted when the party ends, and anything left behind is deleted the next
  time the app opens. Songs that have already played are cleared within about 30
  minutes. It is never backed up.
- Songs protected by Apple Music can't be shared, and CrowdBoombox doesn't try.

## Aux input and the microphone

A host can play music from an aux input, such as an iPod or other MP3 player plugged into the host's phone.
iOS treats any audio input as a microphone, so the app asks for microphone permission
the first time you use the aux input.

- CrowdBoombox records only the aux input, never the built-in microphone, and only while
  the aux input is playing. iOS shows its recording indicator whenever it is on.
- The recording is sent live to the phones in the party and deleted from every phone as
  soon as the aux input stops playing.
- You can change microphone permission at any time in
  **Settings > CrowdBoombox > Microphone**.

## Information we collect

None. CrowdBoombox does not collect names, contact details, music, recordings, location,
device identifiers, usage data, or diagnostics. It contains no third-party code that
does.

Your listening time and party count are kept on your phone only and are never sent
anywhere. The app may keep a short technical log on your phone to help with problems; it
never leaves the phone unless you choose to share it.

## Tips and courts

Tips and courts (the app's color themes) are optional in-app purchases processed by
Apple. We never see your payment details. Which courts you own is confirmed with Apple
and remembered on your phone, along with the court you picked; Restore Purchases asks
Apple again. Choosing a court also changes the app's icon on your phone. Apple's privacy
policy covers these purchases.

## Links and other apps

The app can show other apps by the same developer, using public information from the
App Store, and links to the developer's website and YouTube channel. Those open outside
the app and have their own privacy policies.

## Children's privacy

CrowdBoombox is not directed at children under 13. Because it collects no personal
information from anyone, it collects none from children.

## Information shared with Apple

Apple may provide developers with aggregate, anonymous App Store and crash information
when you have chosen to share it with app developers in your device's settings. This is
controlled by you and by Apple's privacy policy, not by CrowdBoombox.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new
effective date. If a future version of CrowdBoombox ever needs to collect information,
the policy will be updated before that version is released.
